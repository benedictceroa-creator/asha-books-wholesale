Frequently Asked Questions

International Orders — FAQs

Can Asha Books ship Bibles to the UAE?

Yes. We regularly supply Bibles to churches and ministries in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, and across the UAE. We supply English Bibles as well as Telugu and Tamil Bibles for the large Indian Christian community there. Contact us to discuss shipping options and timelines for your order.

Do you supply Bibles to Indian churches in the USA and Australia?

Yes — Indian diaspora churches across the US (New Jersey, Texas, California, Georgia) and Australia (Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Perth) regularly order English, Telugu, and Tamil Bibles from us in bulk. Whether it's for a church anniversary, outreach programme, or mission trip back to India, get in touch and we'll put together a quote.

How does international shipping work for Bible orders?

We recommend international shipping options based on your order size, destination, and timeline. Surface freight is cost-effective for large orders; air freight is faster for urgent needs. We'll include shipping cost estimates in your quote so you can make an informed decision. Import duties and customs clearance are the responsibility of the buyer, and requirements vary by country.

What is the minimum order for international wholesale?

Our standard minimum order is ₹25,000 (approximately US$300 / AED 1,100) for wholesale pricing. For international orders, we may be flexible depending on your shipping destination and total order value — contact us to discuss your specific needs.

How do I pay for an international order?

We accept payment via international wire transfer or SWIFT. Our team will provide full bank details along with your quote. Payment is typically required before dispatch for international orders.

What English Bible translations are available for international orders?

Can I order both English and Indian language Bibles together?

Yes — many of our international customers order a mix of English Bibles and Indian language Bibles in the same shipment. Simply include the details of everything you need in your quote request and we'll put together a combined price and shipping quote.
